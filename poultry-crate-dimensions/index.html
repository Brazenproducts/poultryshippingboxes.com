Frequently Asked Questions

What are standard broiler crate dimensions?

600×400×280mm L×W×H. Standard for 4–6 lb birds at 6–10 per crate across most US commercial integrators.

What are standard turkey crate dimensions?

800×600×400mm for 20–40 lb birds, 2–4 per crate.

Are custom dimensions available?

Yes. Custom dimensions on container-load orders. Custom tooling may apply.

How tall is a standard poultry crate?

280mm (broiler) to 400mm (turkey). Measured to top of crate.

How many crates fit in a 40HQ container?

Depends on crate size — broiler: approximately 400–600 units; turkey: approximately 200–300 units.
