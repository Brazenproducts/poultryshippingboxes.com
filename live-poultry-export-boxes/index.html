The Complete Guide to Poultry Shipping Boxes

Polypropylene corrugated poultry shipping boxes represent the most significant upgrade available to procurement teams still running single-use cardboard. The material is waterproof, structurally consistent, forklift-compatible, and designed for 50-100 reuse cycles. For industries in poultry processing, meat packing, cold chain, the switch from cardboard to PP corrugated typically pays for itself within one production season.

This guide covers everything buyers need to know about sourcing, specifying, and getting maximum value from poultry shipping boxes: material specs, industry applications, import duty considerations, and how to evaluate suppliers in a market full of resellers moving low-quality imports.
